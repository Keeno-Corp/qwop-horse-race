# Design doc: Photo Finish Fiasco
## Core loop
Countdown 3-2-1, then race 60 m; hold leg keys in rhythm to gallop; face-plants ragdoll then auto-right after ~2 s; first nose over line wins; photo-finish replay, results with face-plant counts; R restarts.
## Controls
Solo: Q W O P = FL FR BL BR. Key 2 / button: 2-player, P1 Q W E R, P2 U I O P, + 2 AI. Space skips replay. R restarts.
## Progression
Single race, restart.
## Win and lose
First nose across the line; results podium + most-face-plants award.
## Levels
One straight dirt track, 4 lanes ~2 m apart.
## Feel
Slapstick; popups FACE-PLANT!, PHOTO FINISH!; dust bursts; 0.25x replays.
## Audio
fx.js sfx: crowd cheer/gasp, thud, countdown blips.
## UI
Lane chips, progress bar, popups, results.
## Decisions
- Tone: slapstick over competitive. Asked.
